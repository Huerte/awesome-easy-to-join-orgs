# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience or background, and whether or not they code.

- Be respectful, especially with people making their first contribution
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination
- No spam or ads
- Do not share other people's private information

Comments and contributions that break these rules may be removed. To report something, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, or contact the repository owner through their profile.

Based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
